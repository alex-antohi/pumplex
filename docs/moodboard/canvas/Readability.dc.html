What the boards show

Outline disappears on dark groundsThe #0b0414 ink line is only 1.0 to 1.2:1 against the dark bg, panels and stage, so on dark the silhouette comes from skin and shirt only. On light grounds and the buttons it reads strongly.
Panel borders don’t matchIn the app’s dark theme, panel borders are light (#f6eeff, the theme’s ink). Moodboard v0.1 draws panels with dark #0b0414 borders. Pick one before building game UI.
Width budgetOn a 390 dp screen the body takes 40% of the width at 3×, 53% at 4× and 67% at 5×. At 5× nothing fits beside it.
