1 · The action is the setNo + SET button. Each area has one thing to hit: a chest, a wall, a barrel, a punch machine, an ice block. A dashed ring and a bobbing hint show what to tap. One tap = one working set.
2 · The world keeps the countThe result stays in the scene: loot on the sand, chalk on the wall, Xs on the sign, tickets on the floor, flags up the slope. The scene shakes, a burst says +1 SET, and every 5th gets its own stamp.
3 · Reference, not the appThese boards show the look and the behaviour for the Flutter build. Shapes are placeholders for pixel-art scenes. In the app, a long-press on the object takes the last set back (UNDO here).
