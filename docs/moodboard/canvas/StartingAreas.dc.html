1 · Light floor, dark skyEvery area puts a pale floor (sand, flagstone, rust, concrete, snow) right behind the lifter. That fixes the readability finding: the #0b0414 outline had nothing to stand against on the dark grounds.
2 · One tap, one markA mark lands in about 200 ms with hard-stepped motion, no easing. Marks group in fives so you can count them without numbers. A long-press takes the last one back (UNDO stands in for it here).
3 · Neon means rewardSkies and walls stay dark. Only the marks, the props that react (torches, the neon sign) and the lifter’s clothes get neon. The pearl and the X colours borrow the rarity ladder.
