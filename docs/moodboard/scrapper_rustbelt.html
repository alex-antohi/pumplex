How it's built. Each scene is a 172 × 224 canvas painted pixel by pixel with flat three-tone colours and a 1 px ink line, shown at 2×. Ambient loops tick on a 100 ms hard step. The lifter uses the idle and PR-flex frames from animation/female_fat0_muscle0 (closest body to the Scrapper's F 0/25 until class outfits exist). Light floor behind the lifter, dark sky above, neon only on reactive props and records. With reduced motion turned on, ambient loops and explosions stop; taps still count and the props still answer.
