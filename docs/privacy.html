PUMPLEX Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Summary: PUMPLEX keeps all your data on your phone. The app does not collect, send or share your data. It has no account, no ads and no analytics.

1. Who we are

PUMPLEX is a workout app for Android. An independent developer makes it. In this policy, "we" means the developer of PUMPLEX.

2. Data the app keeps on your phone

To work, the app saves this data in its private storage on your phone:

  • Your character choices, for example class, looks, difficulty and area.
  • The height and body weight that you enter, and changes to them.
  • Your workouts: dates, exercises and sets.
  • Your favorite exercises, your level and your progress.

This data stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and we do not receive it.

3. Data we collect

We collect no data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. It does not ask you for any permissions.

4. Backup files

When the app upgrades data from an older version, it can ask you to save a backup file. The app uses the Android file screen for this. You choose where the file goes. We do not receive the file. If you share the file with other people or services, their privacy rules apply.

5. Android backup

If backup is on in your phone settings, Android can include the app data in your device backup. Google keeps this backup in your Google account, under Google's privacy policy. We cannot see your backup. You can turn backup off in your phone settings.

6. How to delete your data

To delete all app data, uninstall the app. You can also go to Settings → Apps → PUMPLEX → Storage and tap "Clear data". We keep no copy, so we cannot restore deleted data.

7. Children

PUMPLEX is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children, and the app collects no data from anyone.

8. Health information

PUMPLEX is not a medical app and does not give medical advice. Talk to a doctor before you start a new training program.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will put the new version on this page and change the effective date.
