# 01 — App name

Date: 21 November 2026. Status: draft v1. Owner: naming lead.
Input: `docs/app-brief.md`, `docs/moodboard/README.md`.

**Decided (D37, 22 Nov 2026): the app name is PUMPLEX.** This file is the name research from before
that decision. SWOLEMANCER below is a candidate that was not picked. It can stay as a fantasy word in a
tagline (for example the class-step kicker "THE SWOLEMANCER'S PATHS"), but never as the app name.

## 1. Summary

| Rank | Name | Tagline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **SWOLEMANCER** | "Summon your gains." |
| 2 | **BRAWNADO** | "All brawn. No spreadsheets." |
| 3 | **PUMPLEX** (keep) | "Train, not track." |

Runner-up: **SWOLE SAGA**.

Read section 5 for the reasons. All checks are best effort from web search and
public domain lookups on 21 November 2026. They are **not** a legal trademark
search. Ask a trademark lawyer before you launch.

## 2. The working name: PUMPLEX

| Test | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Memorable | Good | Short, two syllables, hard "X" ending. Looks good in Anton caps. |
| Spelling | Good | People spell it the way they hear it. Small risk: "Pumplecks", "Pump Lex". |
| Meaning | Weak | "Pump" is clear. "-plex" says cinema (multiplex), "complex" or Plex (media app). It does not say "game" or "fun". |
| Fits the tone | Medium | Loud, but it sounds like a supplement or a gym chain more than a video game. |
| Store conflicts | Good | No app with this name on Google Play or the App Store. But the "Pump" space is busy: Pumpy (XP, chests), Pumpling (workout pets), Pump Club. |
| Trademark | Good | No "PUMPLEX" mark found. Many "PUMP" marks exist in fitness (PUMPY, IPUMP, PUMPZ FITNESS), so the mark is not very strong. |
| Domains | Medium | `pumplex.com` is taken (since 2012, no visible site). `pumplex.app` and `pumplex.gg` look free. |
| Other languages | Good | No bad meaning found. In German, "pumpen" is gym slang, which helps. |

**Verdict:** a safe, usable name with no hard conflict. Its weak point is meaning:
it does not tell a gamer "this is a game". Keep it if the team values the
work done so far (brief, mood board, app id). Change it if you want the name
to sell the game layer.

Note: you can use "™" on an unregistered name. Do not use "®" until the mark is registered.

## 5. Top 3

### 1. SWOLEMANCER — "Summon your gains."

- It is the most unique name: no app, no brand and no web use found.
- It says "fantasy RPG" and "muscle" in one word. Gamers get the joke fast.
- It fits the class system. You can call the classes "the swolemancer's paths".
- It fits the Borderlands-style tone: loud, a bit stupid, very proud of itself.
- Cons: `.com` is taken (no visible site), so use `swolemancer.app` or `.gg`.
  "Swole" is US slang. Some "SWOLE" marks exist, so do a lawyer check.
- Other taglines: "Dark arts. Big arms." / "Raise the dead. Then raise the bar."

### 2. BRAWNADO — "All brawn. No spreadsheets."

- It is the cleanest for launch: `.com`, `.app` and `.gg` all look free.
- It is a three-way pun: brawn + bravado + tornado. It sounds over the top.
- It is easy to say in English, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese.
- The tagline attacks the "boring tracker" problem that the audience hates.
- Cons: it says "loud and strong", but not "game". The art and the store
  screenshots must do that work. "Brawn" is also the name of a strength app.
- Other taglines: "A storm of gains." / "Pure brawn. Zero math."

### 3. PUMPLEX (keep) — "Train, not track."

- It has no hard conflict in the stores or in trademarks.
- The team already uses it in the brief, the mood board and the app.
- "Train, not track." is a strong, clear promise. It works with any name.
- Cons: weak meaning (see section 2). `.com` is taken.
- Other tagline: "Count sets. Get swole. Level up."

**Runner-up: SWOLE SAGA** ("Every set is a chapter."). The domains look
free and it sounds like a game series. It is less unique, and "Saga" can bring
a fight with King.

## 6. Next steps

1. Check the Instagram, TikTok, Reddit and YouTube handles by hand for the top 3.
2. Run a full search in USPTO, EUIPO (TMview) and WIPO (Global Brand Database), classes 9, 28 and 41.
3. Test the top 3 with 5–10 people from the audience. Ask: "What kind of app is this?"
4. After the decision, buy the domains and set up the handles. (This doc does not register anything.)
5. **Decided:** PUMPLEX (D37). Do steps 1, 2 and 4 for PUMPLEX only.
